About Filmopicks

Filmopicks is a movie and TV show recommendation platform created to help users easily discover titles that suit their preferences. It provides a visually engaging and organized space where users can browse by genre, explore details such as synopsis, cast, and release year, and even submit requests to add, edit, or delete titles from the system.

Students developed this website as part of the Web Programming (WEBPROG) course requirement at the College of Computer Studies, Tarlac State University. It serves as a demonstration of the students’ understanding of website design, structure, and styling using HTML and CSS. The development team focused on creating a layout that balances simplicity, functionality, and aesthetics to ensure an enjoyable browsing experience for users.

The goal of Filmopicks is to connect users to a broad selection of movies and TV shows while showcasing how a web-based recommendation platform can be both user-friendly and well-designed. Future updates may include more interactive features, such as account login, real-time recommendations, and database integration to enhance usability and personalization further.

Help & Support

This section provides answers to common questions and guides users on how to make the most out of Filmopicks. Below are some helpful tips and FAQs to assist you in navigating the website efficiently.

Browsing Movies and Shows

Visit the Movie List page to explore the full collection of available titles. You can use the genre filter to display movies and shows that fit your interests.

Viewing Details

Clicking a movie or show will take you to its Details Page, where you can find information such as synopsis, cast, release year, and recommendations for similar titles.

Submitting Requests

If you would like to suggest adding a new title or modifying an existing one, go to the Submit a Request page. Fill in the form fields accordingly and specify whether the request is for addition, update, or deletion.

User Profile

Access your Profile Page to view your account information, watchlist, and submitted requests. This section allows you to manage your personal preferences and review your activity on the website.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

“Can I make a request without logging in?”

No, Some features may require account access to verify your submissions and track their progress.

“How can I edit or cancel a request?”

Requests can be modified or withdrawn through the Request History tab in your profile.

“Who reviews the movie requests?”

The admins review submitted requests and decide whether to approve, update, or remove the entry.

If you need additional help or have specific concerns not covered here, please reach out through our Contact Information.
